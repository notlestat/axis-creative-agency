## Who, context and purchase situation

H01: the relevant situation is the moment between deciding to work and beginning, regardless of job title or demographic. Age, income and segment size UNKNOWN.

## Pain, desire and identity

H01: organisation can feel like progress without the risk of producing imperfect work. H06: identity as an organised person may make a messy first attempt uncomfortable. These are candidate explanations, not established facts.

## Anxieties, motivations and expectations

Possible anxieties H06 include doing the wrong task or doing it badly. Alternate explanation H07: people may be waiting for information or simply overloaded. The campaign must not blame a person for external constraints.

## Observed language with source IDs

No observed customer language is available. Phrases in the concepts are authored advertising copy, not quotes from research participants.

## Inferences and competing hypotheses

H01 predicts extra planning just before difficult work; H07 predicts delays until a missing dependency is resolved. These explanations produce different creative and product implications.

## Validation and sampling limits

Proposed validation: ask a small varied set of potential users to recount their most recent delayed task, show the artefacts if they consent, and observe a next-task choice. Sampling would be exploratory, not representative. No interviews have occurred.
