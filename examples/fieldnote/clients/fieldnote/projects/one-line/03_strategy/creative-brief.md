# Creative brief

Read client knowledge/learnings.md before drafting. An insight explains a tension in behaviour; a demographic description is not an insight.

## Product

MOCK M01: Fieldnote One Line, a small tear-off desk pad with one task line per sheet. Actual design, dimensions and proof UNKNOWN.

## Audience

HYPOTHESIS H01: people at the point of starting a task who may prefer arranging the work to risking an imperfect attempt. No demographic claim.

## Business objective

MOCK objective: create qualified consideration for a simple paper product. Sales target, conversion rate and commercial viability UNKNOWN.

## Communication objective

Move the audience from thinking they need a better preparation ritual to considering one modest next action. This is a proposed communication shift.

## Problem

H02: the product can look like just another planning accessory. Advertising that celebrates pristine organisation could reinforce the very behaviour it seeks to question.

## Desire

HYPOTHESIS: feel less ceremonious about beginning and more accepting of imperfect work. No mental-health outcome is claimed.

## Tension

H01: I want to make progress, but preparing to make progress can be more comfortable than doing the first imperfect thing. The product itself risks joining the preparation ritual.

## Insight

UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS H01: for some people, an elaborate plan offers the satisfaction of progress before their work has faced reality. It is an explanation to test, not a demographic fact or proven insight.

## Positioning

MOCK proposed positioning: a small paper cue for choosing one next action. No claim of unique invention, greater productivity or superiority to ordinary paper.

## Reason to believe

M01 supports only the fictional physical format, one task line per sheet. Real product imagery and specifications are required before demonstration. No efficacy proof exists.

## Single-minded proposition

One line is enough to name a start. This describes the assumed format and desired action, not a promised outcome.

## Mandatories

Product role must be visible and causal. Warm humour; no shaming, invented urgency, customer quotes or productivity numbers. Retain MOCK labels and unknowns. Human picks the concept.

## Channel

MOCK planning channel: Meta/Instagram paid social in the UK. Actual placement and delivery specs will need current official checks before production.

## Format

MOCK target: one 15s vertical film concept with a static extension. This is a planning duration, not a claimed Melius capability.

## Desired response

A small laugh of recognition, followed by curiosity about the pad and its use. Hypothetical CTA: See One Line. No destination website exists.

## Evidence, assumptions and measurement

Client learnings read before strategy: none exist. Evidence: M01-M05 mock premises; H01/H02/H04/H07/H08 untested. Before real work, test spontaneous comprehension and whether the idea feels useful rather than judgmental. CTR, CPA, ROAS and hold rate UNKNOWN.
