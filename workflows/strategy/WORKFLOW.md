# Creative strategy

Read client `knowledge/learnings.md` before doing strategy. Read intake, research summary and source entries supporting the candidate tension. Run `prepare <client> <campaign> strategy`. Use `skills/axis-positioning/SKILL.md` only if the market frame or differentiated promise needs work.

Complete every section of `03_strategy/creative-brief.md`. Link reason-to-believe claims to evidence IDs. Separate the business result from the change the communication must make in the audience. Distil one single-minded proposition; identify the product's credible role in resolving the tension.

An insight explains a contradiction, not just a demographic or preference. Test it against another explanation and identify what would disprove it. State whether it is validated, inferred or a hypothesis. For real work, unresolved pivotal claims or an unsupported core insight require research/validation before concepting. For the explicitly fictional mock, retain HYPOTHESIS labels and proceed only for demonstration.

Set READY_FOR_REVIEW after the brief is complete, then use the creative workflow. Do not let positioning or copy frameworks produce the campaign idea in place of the creative director.

For Meta/Instagram placement or creative-test planning, consult the workspace's `.local/skills/axis-meta-context/SKILL.md` as a supporting reference. If absent, the optional `tools/install_meta_context.py --root <workspace>` fetches the pinned internal dependency. Keep the strategy lead in charge. Read only creative-format or diagnostic sections, never account/launch automation. Confirm current placement specs with official Meta documentation before production and record the checked date. This does not authorise ad spend.
