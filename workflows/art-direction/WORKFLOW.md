# Art direction

Run status and require current concept approval. Run `prepare <client> <campaign> art-direction`. Read only the selected concept and `skills/axis-visual-image/SKILL.md`, plus relevant research/brand references.

Build art-direction.md and reference-plan.md using the complete templates. Describe the emotion through observable staging, materials, camera, colour, light, action and sound. Specify product geometry/packaging/colour details from supplied evidence. Avoid neon, cyberpunk, smoke, particles, flares and slow motion unless justified by the idea.

References need an exact role: identity, product accuracy, composition, lighting, palette, material or motion. Describe what can be studied and what must not be copied. Missing reference assets remain a gap; do not pretend a written search request is an inspected reference.

This phase produces the treatment and reference plan. It does not generate images or footage. Present the treatment and ask for `APPROVE ART DIRECTION`. STOP before storyboard work until approved.
