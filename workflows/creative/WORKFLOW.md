# Creative direction and concept approval

Read `skills/axis-creative-director/SKILL.md`, then the specific methodology references it routes to. Use the creative brief and evidence as inputs. Run `prepare <client> <campaign> concepts`.

Explore multiple mechanisms, then develop approximately five strong and genuinely different campaign territories. Use structural, associative and inversion methods. Record which method changed the idea. The deliverable is five fully considered ideas, not five headlines for one execution.

Complete concept-01 through concept-05, including all requested fields, ten 1-10 scores with reasons, closest reference mechanics, originality limitations and a pre-mortem. Check the relevant campaign library cases before claiming originality. The library is a calibration aid; verify original sources before presenting campaign details as client research. Its coverage is not an exhaustive originality clearance.

Challenge the favourite with a kill-your-darlings pass. Refine the weakest useful criterion, without inflating scores to hit a target. Apply `skills/axis-performance-creative/SKILL.md` after ideation for audience understanding, product role, hook, proof and action. Keep the Big Idea intact.

Complete `concept-comparison.md` with summary, strengths, weaknesses, ten scores and recommendation. Present all five to the user, then STOP for an explicit human concept decision. Do not write art direction or record an approval from your own recommendation. `Develop concept 3` asks for refinement; it does not automatically authorise choosing concept 3 as the winner.
