# Client intake

Run `create-client <slug> --name <name>` and `start-campaign <client> <campaign> --name <name>` when absent. Collect the fields in `templates/client.yaml` and `templates/briefs/intake.md`. Save supplied logos, guidelines, product images and references in the client's `brand/` folders. Preserve originals and note usage restrictions.

Use prior client facts before asking again. Record source and date for product facts, claims, pricing, geography, tone and restrictions in `knowledge/brand.md`. Unknown facts stay UNKNOWN, empty lists mean not supplied. Do not derive a brand website from a name.

Fill `01_intake/intake.md`. Distinguish business objective, conversion action and communication objective. Read the actual offer, product and audience before proceeding. If critical product, audience or deliverable information is missing, ask focused questions; complete what can be completed. Set READY_FOR_REVIEW when intake is usefully complete, with gaps explicit.
